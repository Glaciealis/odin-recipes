Chocolate Brownies

Description

Delicious, moist and chewy brownies.
Recipe source.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup self-rising flour
  • 1/3 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts (optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C)
  2. Grease and flour an 8x8 or 9x9 inch baking pan.
  3. Beat together sugar and butter in a medium bowl.
  4. Add eggs into sugar mixture and mix well.
  5. Combine flour, cocoa, and salt; stir into sugar mixture.
  6. Mix in vanilla, then stir in walnuts. Spread evenly into prepared pan.
  7. Bake in the preheated oven until edges are firm, 25-30 minutes. Cool before cutting into squares.
