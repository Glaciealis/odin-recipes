Description

An modern-styled parfait containing alternating layers of cereals, fruits, and yogurt.
Recipe source.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups vanilla yogurt
  • 1 cup granola
  • Any fruit(s) of your choice.

Steps

  1. Layer some granola into a glass cup.
  2. Layer some yogurt on top of the granola.
  3. Layer some fruits on top of the yogurt.
  4. Repeat the layering in the same order until the fruit is at the top of the cup.
